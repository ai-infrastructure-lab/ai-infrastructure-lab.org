Initiated by Andreas Liesenfeld and Mark Dingemanse in 2022, the European Open-Source AI Index conducts research on open-source AI, publishes technology assessments, and hosts an open, community-driven database with information on model openness, licensing, and EU regulation of generative AI systems and providers.

More information [here](https://osai-index.eu).
