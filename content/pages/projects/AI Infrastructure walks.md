AI infrastructure walks serve as a participatory research method that allows communities to collectively observe, map, and critically reflect on the physical footprint of digital technologies. Work-in-progress is currently under way to design these methods for diverse audiences, ranging from high school students to domain experts. The initiative aims to develop a robust participatory framework to study emerging data transfer, processing, and storage infrastructures across Europe. To ground this research, preliminary data walks have already taken place in Frankfurt, Karlsruhe, and Kleve (Germany), as well as in Copenhagen (Denmark) and Amsterdam (Netherlands).
